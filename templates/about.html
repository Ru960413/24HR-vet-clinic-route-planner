This map began on a night I still remember — my dog was in trouble, and as I held her I dug through search results that offered nothing but outdated listings and clinics with no emergency service at all. That helplessness is something I never want another pet parent to feel.

So I built the 24HR Emergency Vet Map: emergency vet clinics across Taiwan, one-tap navigation and calling, after-hours notes — and filters so that parents of birds, turtles, rodents, rabbits and reptiles can find clinics that will actually see them. It's free, no login, and continuously updated.

Your support goes toward: keeping the site and map services running, ongoing verification of clinic information, and more small tools for animals in the future.

The rest of the time, I'm an intuitive artist and animal communicator — painting soul portraits of animals, and doing practical things for them too. My work lives at soulbrush.art 🎨

Thank you for stopping by, for sharing your life with an animal, and for every cup of matcha that keeps this map glowing 🍵🐾
