So I built the 24HR Emergency Vet Map: emergency vet clinics across Taiwan, one-tap navigation and calling, after-hours notes — and filters so that parents of birds, turtles, rodents, rabbits and reptiles can find clinics that will actually see them. It's free, no login, and continuously updated.

資料是怎麼來的？多久更新一次？
以 Google 商家資料自動比對為底，每一間再逐一人工查證（清單頁標 ✓ 的就是近期查證過的）。系統每個月會自動掃描全台，偵測歇業、搬家和新開的急診醫院。
為什麼不直接搜 Google Maps 就好？
直接搜「24小時獸醫」會混入很多「24 小時住院照護但沒有急診」的診所，甚至明寫「無急診服務」的也會出現。而急診真正需要的資訊——要不要先打電話、急診專線、掛號費、特寵收不收——Google 上大多沒有，這裡都幫你查證註記好了。
發現資訊有誤，或想推薦沒收錄的醫院？
用回報表單告訴我，查證後就會更新，感謝每一位幫忙的飼主 🙏

Where does the data come from? How often is it updated?
Google business data provides the base, and every clinic is then verified by hand (the ✓ marks on the list page show recent verification). An automated monthly sweep detects closures, relocations and newly opened emergency clinics.
Why not just search Google Maps?
A raw search for "24hr vet" mixes in clinics that offer 24-hour inpatient care but no emergency service — some even state "no emergency service". And what an emergency actually requires — whether to call first, dedicated emergency lines, registration fees, whether exotic pets are seen — mostly isn't on Google. It's verified and noted here.
Spotted an error, or know a clinic that's missing?
Tell me via the report form — it gets verified and updated. Thank you 🙏
