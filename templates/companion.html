I found animal Reiki because of my black Shiba, Hei Hei — in 2024, she was diagnosed with a bladder tumor.

She had minimally invasive surgery at the end of 2024, but follow-ups showed the tumor growing, so she went on chemotherapy medication. Her body has always been sensitive to medicine, and after a while the side effects set in. I started cooking fresh food for her — and that was also when I found Reiki. My very first Reiki booking was for her. She has since recovered, and I went on to train in Reiki myself.

What I offer here is companionship on the energy level — never a substitute for veterinary care, but a way to let the animals we love feel how much we love them, and that we want to walk through this stretch with them. If that kind of companionship speaks to you, you're welcome to visit my site.
