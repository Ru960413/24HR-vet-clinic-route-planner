{{ t.snakebite_sub }}——先深呼吸。被咬之後你有時間，但沒有可以浪費的時間。

現在就做的三件事

  1. 保持毛孩安靜，不要讓牠奔跑。抱得動就抱著走，傷口位置盡量低於心臟。
  2. 記下蛇的樣子。安全距離內用手機拍一張最好——顏色、花紋、頭型。不要抓蛇、不要追蛇。血清分種類，認得出蛇就是幫牠搶時間。
  3. 先打電話，再出發。打給下方名單的醫院，講三件事：被蛇咬、蛇長什麼樣（或拍到的照片）、多久能到。確認對方此刻有血清、收蛇咬，再上路。

不要做：不割開傷口、不用嘴吸毒、不冰敷、不綁止血帶——這些都已證實會讓傷害更嚴重。

台灣的毒蛇與血清分型

獸醫端的抗蛇毒血清分三種，對應不同的蛇——這就是為什麼「記下蛇的樣子」重要：

🟤 龜殼花・赤尾青竹絲（出血性毒）
最常見的咬傷來源，共用同一型血清。全台儲備最多的一型。
⚫ 雨傘節・眼鏡蛇（神經性毒）
共用同一型血清。雨傘節毒性強但咬傷初期症狀不明顯——懷疑被咬就直奔醫院，不要等腫起來。
🟠 鎖鏈蛇（混合毒）
主要出沒於南部與東部（高屏、花東）。極罕見，但全台獸醫端血清儲備只有個位數——偏偏多在資源較少的區域發生。在這些地區被咬，第一通電話就說「懷疑是鎖鏈蛇」，讓醫院提早確認血清與轉診路線。
其他蛇種（如百步蛇）
目前全聯會系統沒有對應的獸醫用血清儲備。立刻電話聯絡最近的急診獸醫院，描述蛇的樣子，依獸醫師指示處理。

Three things to do right now

  1. Keep your animal calm and still — no running. Carry them if you can, keeping the wound below heart level.
  2. Remember what the snake looked like. A phone photo from a safe distance is best — color, pattern, head shape. Never try to catch or chase it. Antivenom is type-specific; identifying the snake buys time.
  3. Call before you go. Call a hospital from the list below and say three things: snakebite, what the snake looked like, and how soon you can arrive. Confirm they have antivenom in stock right now before you set off.

Do not: cut the wound, suck out venom, apply ice, or use a tourniquet — all proven to make things worse.

Taiwan's venomous snakes & antivenom types

Veterinary antivenom in Taiwan comes in three types, matched to the snake — which is why remembering the snake matters:

🟤 Taiwan habu & bamboo viper (hemorrhagic)
The most common bites; one shared antivenom, and the most widely stocked.
⚫ Banded krait & Chinese cobra (neurotoxic)
One shared antivenom. Krait bites can look deceptively mild at first — if you suspect one, go now; don't wait for swelling.
🟠 Russell's viper (mixed venom)
Found mainly in the south and east (Kaohsiung-Pingtung, Hualien-Taitung). Very rare, but only a handful of doses exist in the whole veterinary system — mostly in regions with the fewest resources. If bitten there, say "suspected Russell's viper" in your first call so the hospital can confirm stock and referral routes early.
Other snakes (e.g. the hundred-pacer)
No veterinary antivenom is currently stocked for these. Call the nearest emergency vet immediately, describe the snake, and follow the vet's guidance.

About the cost, upfront

One dose of antivenom is NT$28,000 (set nationally by the veterinary association; treatment and hospitalization are extra), and serious bites can need more than one. No hospital is overcharging you — it is a controlled drug, the same price everywhere. If you ever stand at a counter at 3 a.m. holding your animal, we want you to be someone who already knew this number. They are worth it, and you deserve to know first.

On a weekday, in daylight

Weekdays 09:00–18:00, any vet hospital can request antivenom through its county veterinary association — so for daytime bites, call the vet you trust first. The request channel closes at night and on holidays; that is when the night list above matters.
